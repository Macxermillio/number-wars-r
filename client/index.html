How to play

Beginner guide — learn in about 2 minutes

flag Goal — two ways to win

You and your opponent each start with 8 pieces (numbered 1–8). Win by capturing all enemy pieces (or by points if no one can finish the fight).

🥇 OR collect 10 ⭐ stars. Be the first to gather ten stars and you win instantly — a fast comeback path when you're behind on pieces.

playing_cards 1. Your pieces — what the numbers mean

⭐ Strength = attack

The big number. Higher hits harder. Max is 8.

🛡 Armor = health shield

Damage hits armor first. Small pieces (1–3) are tanks with lots of armor.

⚡ Spike = counter-attack

Hurts anyone who attacks this piece. Collected from ⚡ shards.

📏 Range = steps per turn

A 5 can move up to 5 squares. You may always move fewer — or just reposition.

💡 Simple rule of thumb: small numbers = tough defenders, big numbers = fragile attackers. Hide your big pieces behind small ones.

footprints 2. Moving — rooks vs queens

  • ♟Pieces 1–4 move like a queen (straight + diagonal).
  • ♜Pieces 5–8 move like a rook (straight lines only).
  • 🚧You can't jump over pieces or dark brick walls.
  • ⚔️Move onto an enemy to attack it — on every turn, no matter the bonus effect.

casino 3. Each turn has a bonus power

Every turn the game rolls one bonus. You can always ignore it and just move.

➕
Merge — combine two of yours (automatic)

Move one of your pieces onto another of yours. Strengths and armor add up (max 8 strength). This is how you build a super-piece.

➗
Split — one piece becomes two (click effect, then a piece)

Only even pieces (2, 4, 6, 8) can split — an 8 becomes two 4s on adjacent squares.

➖
Weaken — shrink any piece by 1 (click effect, then a piece)

Works on yours or the enemy's. Drops strength, armor and range by 1 — spikes are untouched, never reduced. Lower strength raises the spike cap by 1, so spikes are never shed. A 1/1/1 piece is invulnerable.

✖️
Strengthen — double attack, lose armor (rare!)

A 3 becomes a 6 but loses 3 armor. Never use it on a piece with no armor left.

swords 4. Fighting — what happens when you attack

  1. Spikes hit first. The defender's ⚡ chips your armor, then your strength. Weak attackers can die on spikes!
  2. Overwhelm wins instantly. If your (remaining) strength beats their strength + armor + spikes combined, you capture them.
  3. Otherwise you chip their armor and bounce back. Hit the same piece twice to finish it.

💡 Beginner tip: attack spiky pieces with your tanky small pieces (they have armor to spare), and save your big 7s and 8s for clean captures.

diamond 5. Pickups, stars & the shrinking board

  • 🛡Armor shards give +1 armor forever (no limit). Always grab them.
  • ⚡Spike shards give +1 counter-attack. Small pieces can hold more spikes than big ones.
  • ⭐Stars — collect 10 to win. One spawns every 10 turns in the center of the board (only one at a time). Land on it to grab it; stars don't block your path. Watch your ⭐ counter (0/10) in the side panel.
  • 🧱After turn 20, dark brick walls appear one per turn. The board slowly closes in — don't hide forever!

rocket_launch Your first game — do this

  1. Move a small piece (1–3) forward as a shield.
  2. Walk onto 🛡 shards with anything, onto ⚡ shards with small pieces, and onto ⭐ stars whenever you can reach one safely.
  3. On a Merge turn, stack two pieces together (3 + 5 = a mighty 8!).
  4. Attack only when your strength looks bigger than their whole pile (strength + armor + spikes).
  5. Keep half an eye on stars — grabbing 10 wins the game even if you're losing the battle.
Tip: you can also just move without using the bonus.
